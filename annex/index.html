Optional historical annex: telecommunications infrastructure and state secrecy

This annex preserves the supplied AT&T and NSA source note beside the circuit record. It provides institutional history for readers interested in how communications infrastructure, government access, and litigation intersected. The events below have their own dates and sources.

Period Documented event Source
1945–1975 Congressional records describe Operation SHAMROCK, in which RCA Global, ITT World Communications, and Western Union International supplied international telecommunications traffic to U.S. intelligence agencies. House committee report; Church Committee hearing
October 1975 A House inquiry took testimony from AT&T and Chesapeake & Potomac Telephone representatives about national-security taps. This is a separate documented event from the carriers named in the SHAMROCK traffic-transfer record. House committee report; Ford Library hearing transcript
1985 onward Documents reported by ProPublica and The New York Times place AT&T in the NSA's FAIRVIEW partnership and describe later equipment in domestic Internet hubs. ProPublica / New York Times investigation
2008–2019 The FISA Amendments Act created a certification-based dismissal route for certain suits against assisting telecommunications providers. The 2019 Jewel v. NSA order granted the defendants summary judgment, citing the national-security disclosure risk of proceeding. 50 U.S.C. § 1885a; 2019 court order
