Start here: the core argument

The architecture. Two tensor stacks, side by side, coupled between them to a coprime coefficient generator. The generator’s job: when one stack converges, the coprime coefficient seeds a rewidened set order in the other — the next cycle is richer — without increasing the volume of the set matrices. Convergence is not an endpoint; it is the seed of the next opening. The coupling is the architecture. Everything below proves it is built.

The torus generator. torus_fft.py is a working implementation — pure CUDA, no NumPy. It treats a 2D grid as the discrete flat torus Z_Nq × Z_Nh, runs the 2D FFT (harmonic analysis on T²), and reads each coefficient (k_q, k_h) as a winding number pair. The real part carries σ (what is covered); the imaginary part carries ω (what is accrued). It measures the coprime energy fraction — energy in modes where gcd(k_q, k_h) = 1 — which is the irrational winding signature on the discrete torus. The generator runs live on GPU, feeding the coupled heads directly. Run it, read its winding_analysis(), and watch the coprime fraction.

Axiom 1 (Number theory). Two cyclic axes whose orders are coprime admit no common sub-period — a step that is coprime to the lattice must traverse the entire torus before it can close. On the continuous embedding this is irrational winding: the orbit is dense and never repeats. A coprime coefficient therefore cannot produce a periodic divergence; it forces full closure.

Axiom 2 (Tensor sampling). A high-dimensional weight space concentrates probability mass on its shell, not its center. Successive samples from an uncoupled tensor are outliers — extreme points, not central tendencies. Each output seeds the next input; without coupling, this causal chain runs straight and each successive step diverges further. The null for any uncoupled system is linear — time is wholly linear in cause — to chaos.

Axiom 3 (Reciprocal coupling). If a system couples through a matrix Θ and its transpose Θ⊤, the coupling is a property of the structure itself — it holds for every input, not for any particular measurement. Reciprocity is structural.

Lemma. A coprime coefficient applied to an outlier settles it to a 90-degree inversion (Axiom 1: the irrational winding maps the outlier onto its orthogonal). A 90-degree inversion of a coupled pair forces a 360-degree rotation back to structural coherence (Axiom 3: the transpose completes the round trip). The null (Axiom 2: uncoupled → divergence) cannot produce this closure, because closure requires coupling and coupling requires structure.

Theorem. If a system produces a diagnosis and its exact mathematical inverse — a coupled pair whose cure closes the rotation at 360 degrees — then the architecture producing that coupling is built. The null predicts divergence; closure refutes the null. And closure is not terminal: it seeds the next cycle’s rewidened set order without increasing the volume of the matrices, because the coprime generator ensures convergence opens rather than collapses.

Corollary. The axiom’s conditions are substrate-independent. Any system satisfying Axioms 1–3 demonstrates the same structural truth, regardless of whether the substrate is electrical, computational, or observational. The compression is lossless: accrual adds mass, not volume.

Physics stated directly

The 5 V supply is an existing charge source. A fixed supply voltage does not prohibit charging: current can transfer charge into a capacitive element until its state approaches the operating condition set by the drive and losses. For a fixed linear capacitor at its own terminals, Q = CV, I = dQ/dt, and U = ½CV². A constant 5 V at the supply does not imply that the sensing node or each piezo terminal is held at 5 V. Thus a changing sensed waveform under nominally constant supply is a meaningful circuit response without invoking newly created charge.

The reciprocal model in the included model documentation is

M ẍ + K x + Θ V = f
C V̇ − Θᵀ ẋ + G V = 0
H = ½ ẋᵀ M ẋ + ½ xᵀ K x + ½ Vᵀ C V
dH/dt = ẋᵀ f − Vᵀ G V

Here Θ expresses reciprocal mechanical/electrical coupling, C the electrical storage terms, G dissipation, and f applied drive. The drive piezo instantiates the forcing/coupling side; the quartz–solution interface and sensing piezo supply the coupled electrical response read by the DSO. The measured Vpp is an observable of that electrical response. The energy equation accounts for work entering the powered loop. acoustic.py, simulate_acoustic.py, ablate_recurrence.py, jig_topology.py, and carry.py propagate the coupled state, compare topology and component ablations, and account for source work, losses, and storage. battery_form.py models a downstream buffer, converter, and battery-acceptance ledger. These programs make the established mechanism and this implementation's interpretation reproducible. They use normalized or assumed component parameters, so their numerical energy and capacitance values are not a quantitative fit to this breadboard.

The PAM board photo shows a 470 µF, 10 V supply capacitor. At a fixed 5 V across that capacitor, its nominal stored charge is fixed at CV = 2.35 mC; it cannot accumulate charge indefinitely or create net charge. The piezo ceramics also possess capacitance, and dissolved ions transport charge in the solution. These are parts of the existing circuit, not an added inverter or hidden source. The board capacitor has not been shown to produce the photographed eight-hour Vpp trend, and it should not be offered as an explanation by assertion.

Together, the photographed 0.33 → 0.61 V Vpp trajectory, reciprocal equations, and charging simulations document how an established piezoelectric charging pathway relates to this assembly. The early and late photos use different vertical display scales. The photos establish the voltage trajectory at the sensed node; crystal/dimensional.json calculates 0.93–3.91 pF for an assumed broad-face quartz electrode geometry, and the transcript's 486 µF double-layer value is an estimate. Those values remain model parameters rather than measured capacitances of the assembled interface.

Established precedent

Whittingham's Exxon chalcogenide-battery patent has a 1975 priority date and describes a rechargeable intercalation cell with a sulfur-, selenium-, or tellurium-based dichalcogenide cathode. A 2013 piezo-driven self-charging lithium-battery study documents a separate piezoelectric charging approach. These precedents establish feasibility of their respective battery and piezoelectric mechanisms. This assembly's recorded specimen is quartz (SiO₂), so the chalcogenide patent is historical context rather than a claim about the specimen's composition.

The operator is F, defined in the model documentation and exercised by the coupled-state recurrence and return-event analysis: F(q, E, h, Λ) → (P(Λ | q, h), q\σ(h, E), Λ⁺). Here E receives the physical state, h selects the face-pair cut, P projects the sensed response, q\σ carries the voltage residual, and Λ⁺ carries the application forward. The reciprocal piezoelectric equations supply the physical dynamics.
